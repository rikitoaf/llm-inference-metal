**Correctness: all checks pass.**
- DFlash2 digests equal production.
- Golden tolerance: cosine at least 0.9998.
- Greedy divergences are near-ties only (margin ≤ 0.14).
- Sampling distribution: Bonferroni p = 1.0.

**Round 1 A/B** (`splash/dev-dspine/dev/ab-round1/`; A = DFlash2 production, B = DSpine block 16, window 8192):

| context | A tok/s | B tok/s | B vs A |
|---|---|---|---|
| sessions, 4–10K (9 prompts × 3 seeds) | 67.5 | 68.1 | +1.0% |
| short agent prompts | 57.7 | 55.7 | −3.4% |
| long, ~70K (2 prompts) | 45.4 | 60.7 | +34% (weak sample) |

Costs and caveats:
- B costs +3.5 ms per cycle and +415 MiB.
- A 2048-token window is worse than DFlash2 at long context (−13%).
- Phase 0's offline +28–39% was a measurement artefact (scoring at DFlash2's cycle boundaries); the port itself
  matches the reference.

**Verdict:** it wins only at long context. The best architecture candidate is a **hybrid**: DFlash2 below about
32K of context, DSpine above it. Round 2 (hybrid switch, a cheaper draft, tree budget) was planned and its scripts
started, but it was halted before any measurement.
